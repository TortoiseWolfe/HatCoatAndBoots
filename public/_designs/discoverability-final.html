How a child discovers it (touch, no hover): the breathing halo + the "👆 try tapping" line pull the eye → first tap opens the tooltip (doesn't remove yet — a tap is never punished) → second tap removes the roof and the sun visibly floods in (the reward). After they've done it twice, single taps just work. Reduced-motion: the halo is a static ring + "👆 tap" badge, no breathing. Every part is a real keyboard button; toggles are announced aloud.
